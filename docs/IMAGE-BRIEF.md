# Photography Shot Brief & Visual Guidelines (`docs/IMAGE-BRIEF.md`)

This photography brief derived from **Drybar Brand Kit Page 09** provides the complete shot list, technical specifications, and visual guidelines for replacing component placeholders with final brand photography.

---

## 1. Visual Aesthetics & Lighting Guidelines

- **Natural & Generous Light**: Soft, diffused natural daylight. Warm color temperature (avoid cold/blue cast).
- **No Harsh Flash**: Avoid hard directional strobe shadows or artificial studio ring-light reflections.
- **Brand Palette Integration**: Ensure natural pops of Drybar Yellow (`#FEDD30`), Butter (`#FDF2C9`), Cream (`#FCF9F2`), and Charcoal (`#262523`) appear naturally in background interiors, styling tools, or apparel.
- **Hair in Motion**: Capture natural movement—a gentle turn, a bounce, hair flowing mid-blowout. Avoid stiff, frozen salon poses.
- **Authentic Expressions**: Relaxed, genuine smiles. High-energy yet effortless atmosphere.
- **Retouching**: Light retouching only. Preserve natural skin textures and healthy hair shine.

---

## 2. Mandatory Consent & Release Requirements

- **Written Release**: Every identifiable model or guest appearing in photography MUST sign a written model release for global digital marketing usage prior to shooting.

---

## 3. Strict "Never-in-Frame" Prohibitions

1. **NO Scissors, Shears, or Razors**: Never show hair cutting tools.
2. **NO Hair Color Bowls, Foils, or Brushes**: Never show tint bowls, bleach foils, or chemical processing gear.
3. **NO Clinical or Medical Equipment**: No trichology scopes, scalp treatment needles, or clinical apparatus.
4. **NO Alcohol or Barware**: No cocktail glasses, shaker tins, or bar counter staging in the Qatar market.
