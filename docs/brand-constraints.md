# Brand Constraints: Hard Build Rules for Drybar Qatar

This document records every hard rule and brand constraint extracted from the official brand source files:
- `brand/Drybar Qatar - Brand & Collaboration Kit.pdf`
- `brand/drybar_qatar_price_list.pdf`
- `brand/Asset_1.svg`
- `brand/services.json`

---

## 1. Core Services & Concept Constraints
- [ ] **Blowouts & Styling Only**: Drybar Qatar provides blowouts and hair styling only. **NEVER** imply, state, or list hair cuts, hair coloring, highlights, or chemical treatments.
- [ ] **No Medical or Hair-Growth Claims**: **NEVER** use words or make claims regarding "repairing damage", "regrowing hair", "curing", or "healing". Use "healthy-looking" or "glossy" instead.

## 2. Qatar Market Sensitivities
- [ ] **No Alcohol or Bar Imagery**: While official trademarked cocktail-inspired style names (e.g. *Half Uptini*, *Uptini*, *Cure Liquor*) are preserved verbatim, **NEVER** build creative, props, icons, sets, or captions around alcohol or bar imagery in the Qatar market.
- [ ] **Modesty & Cultural Appropriateness**: Ensure all imagery, phrasing, and campaign references are modest and locally appropriate.
- [ ] **Sole Publishable Location**: **Gewan Island, The Pearl, Doha** is the only publishable location. Do not mention unopened or unannounced locations.

## 3. Visual & Color Constraints
- [ ] **Yellow Is NEVER For Text**: Drybar Yellow (`#FEDD30`) on Cream background (`#FCF9F2`) provides only ~1.3:1 contrast and fails WCAG AA/AAA guidelines at every text size. Yellow is strictly for surfaces, fills, rules, and accents. Charcoal (`#262523`) carries all text.
- [ ] **No Pure Black**: Body text and dark surfaces must use Charcoal (`#262523`), never pure `#000000`.

## 4. Logo Misuse Rules (Brand Kit Page 07)
- [ ] **Do NOT Recolour or Retint**: Never change logo fills or convert them to arbitrary colors or `currentColor`.
- [ ] **Do NOT Stretch or Skew**: Maintain exact aspect ratio and vector proportions.
- [ ] **Do NOT Alter Typography**: Do not replace, retype, or modify the logotype fonts.
- [ ] **Do NOT Add Effects**: No drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, or gradients on the logo.
- [ ] **Do NOT Place on Low Contrast**: Ensure clear background isolation (minimum required contrast).
- [ ] **Do NOT Rotate**: Always keep logo horizontal and level.
- [ ] **Do NOT Re-proportion**: Keep the relationship between mark and type unaltered.

## 5. Social Channels & Links
- [ ] **Instagram Only**: Instagram (`@thedrybar.qatar`) is the **ONLY** active social channel.
- [ ] **NO Inactive Channels**: Do **NOT** create, link, or display social icons/links for TikTok, Snapchat, or Facebook.
- [ ] **WhatsApp Booking Contact**: WhatsApp (+974 7773 0600) is the official messaging contact alongside Instagram.

## 6. Language & Copy Rules (Bilingual EN / AR)
- [ ] **Do NOT Machine-Translate Arabic**: Arabic copy must be written natively with Gulf warmth, not translated word-for-word.
- [ ] **Keep Trademark Names in Latin**: Keep "Drybar" and all official style names (*Blow Dry*, *Beach Waves*, *Hollywood Waves*, *Retro Waves*, *Half Uptini*, *French Twist*, *Uptini*) in Latin script in both English and Arabic versions. Never transliterate them into Arabic characters.
- [ ] **Proper RTL Layout**: Right-to-left layout must be implemented natively using CSS logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`, etc.), not an English layout with Arabic text dropped in.

## 7. Tone of Voice Word List (Brand Kit Page 10)

| Category | APPROVED Words to USE | PROHIBITED Words to AVOID |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Service Names** | `blowout`, `style`, `bounce`, `shine`, `finish` | `treatment`, `procedure`, `session`, `therapy` |
| **Booking Call** | `book a chair`, `come in`, `pop by`, `an hour that is yours` | `reserve your appointment slot now` |
| **Self-Care** | `we love`, `we are obsessed with` | `pamper yourself`, `indulge`, `treat yourself` |
| **Exclusivity** | *(Warm & inviting)* | `world-class`, `unrivalled`, `elite`, `exclusive` |
| **Hair Benefit** | `healthy-looking`, `glossy` | `repairs damage`, `regrows`, `heals`, `cures` |

## 8. Packages & Memberships Note
- [!] **Clarification on Brand Kit "DO NOT PUBLISH" Box**: Page 12 of the Brand Kit features a "What we ask you not to do" list containing *"Publish package prices, membership tiers..."*. This restriction is addressed to third-party creators, influencers, and media partners, **NOT** to Drybar Qatar's official website.
- [ ] **Website Membership Scope**: Memberships ship on the website exactly as printed on the official price list (`brand/drybar_qatar_price_list.pdf` & `brand/services.json`):
  * **3 Sessions**: 20% OFF your 3rd session
  * **6 Sessions**: 50% OFF your 6th session
  * **8 Sessions**: 50% OFF your 8th session + Complimentary Hair Treatment
